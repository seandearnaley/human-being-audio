## Ligature 2.0.0

- **One window you can read:** a song strip of sections and bridges, a harmony lane with Roman numerals, function words and a tension line, a piano roll for every part with voice-leading lines, a stutter lane, a context inspector, a lit keyboard and a one-line theory lens.
- **Bridges and key changes:** eight route families from common tone to stutter slam, voice-led from one section into the next with every part playing, pivot chords tagged, at 1, 2 or 4 bars.
- **Loop, launch, land:** bridges timed backwards from the Land grid, regenerated shorter when launched late, held bridges that build until release, MIDI-note launches and an automatable Scene parameter.
- **A transport you can trust:** the position comes from the host every block; the preview clock plays while the host is stopped.
- **Parts:** Style, Density and Mutate (Nudge, Twist, Rewrite) on every part, pinned notes that survive mutation, other takes to audition, Rate for patterned styles.
- **Chords:** a three-ring neighbourhood for every chord, voicing, inversion and colour controls, voice-leading across the loop, a progression library in genre packs, one-key chords and played-chord naming.
- **MIDI where you want it:** channels 1-4 from a leader, one part on channel 1 from each follower instance, and drag-out of a section, the song or the last performance.
- **Hear it straight away:** the built-in sound plays every part through the studio's own instruments (Torus Glide, Lineage, Strike Body and Datum), with a lighter sketch synth as the alternative.
- Ligature 1 sessions open on the default song.
