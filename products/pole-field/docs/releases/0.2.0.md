## Pole Field 0.2.0

- Smooth MIDI expression support across the Z-plane filterbank: `CC1` Mod Wheel, `CC74` Slide/Timbre, Channel Pressure & Poly Aftertouch, `CC121` Reset All Controllers, and RPN `00 00` Pitch Bend Sensitivity — designed for expressive live playing or direct pairing with Cutline.
- Live glowing modulation ghost arcs and tip indicators on `Morph X`, `Morph Y`, `Morph Z`, `Freq`, `Resonance`, and `Drive` showing real-time movement from both internal curve lanes and incoming MIDI expression.
- 64-step Undo/Redo (`⌘Z` / `⌘⇧Z` with continuous curve-drag coalescing) and A/B Compare (`Copy A → B` / `Copy B → A`) in the top bar, preserving parameters, custom lane curves, and Burg LPC audio imprints.
- Shared studio `hba::dsp` curve and lane-math engine primitives with zero audio-thread allocations verified under RealtimeSanitizer.
