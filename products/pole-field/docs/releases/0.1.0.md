## Pole Field 0.1.0

- First release of Pole Field: a 14-pole, 14-zero Z-plane morphing filterbank across eight 3D morph cubes with polar log-frequency interpolation and Serial, Hybrid, and Parallel topologies.
- Inter-stage second-order ADAA (`Tube`, `Clip`, `Fold`, `Buchla`, `Sine`) with matched 2×/4×/8× HIIR oversampling, mono Sub Lock crossover with phase `Fit`, MIDI formant pitch tracking, four tempo-locked Bézier movement lanes, and Burg LPC WAV spectral imprinting.
- Available as VST3, macOS Audio Unit (`aumf`), and Standalone with 12 factory presets and eight performance macros.
