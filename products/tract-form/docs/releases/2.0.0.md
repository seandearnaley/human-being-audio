# Tract Form 2.0.0

Tract Form gets a reese core and a real filter, for deep, rolling basses rather than buzz.

- **Four oscillators per voice.** Each reese voice plays Sine (the 1.x sound), Saw, Pulse or PD, a resonant phase distortion in the spirit of the synth the original Reese came from. Shape bends the saw, narrows the pulse or sweeps PD's resonance through the harmonics. The master still phase-modulates every one.
- **Stack.** Up to seven voices per reese voice, spread by Spread and Beat, for anything from a slow two-saw roll to a wall of saws.
- **Filter.** Reach is now the cutoff of a resonant filter that follows the note: Clean (the 1.x low-pass), Ladder, MS-20, Low-pass or Notch, with Resonance and Drive, before or after the fold. Ladder and MS-20 self-oscillate in tune.
- **Movement.** Lanes can now move Shape and Resonance.
- **Presets.** The Reese bank moves to the saw core and the ladder, and four presets are new: Two Saw Roller, Seven Saw Wall, Resonant PD Reese and Squelch Ladder.
- **Interface.** A Filter node sits after Fold. Each Reese card shows its oscillator and Shape, and Master holds Stack and Spread. Mid, Side and Crossover moved into Out.

Changed: the single Shape control became a Shape per voice. Presets and sessions from 1.x load with both voices on their old Shape, but host automation of the old Shape no longer has a target.
