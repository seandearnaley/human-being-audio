## Tract Form 0.3.0

- Smooth MIDI expression support across the synth engine: `CC1` Mod Wheel, `CC74` Slide/Timbre, Channel Pressure & Poly Aftertouch, `CC121` Reset All Controllers, and RPN `00 00` Pitch Bend Sensitivity — ready for expressive controllers or direct pairing with Cutline.
- Live glowing modulation ghost arcs on `Coupling`, `Beat`, `Reach`, `Sculpt`, and `Width` knobs plus live modulation highlights on `Morph` and `Drive` bars showing combined internal lane and MIDI expression movement in real time.
- 64-step Undo/Redo (`⌘Z` / `⌘⇧Z` with continuous curve-drag coalescing) and A/B Compare (`Copy A → B` / `Copy B → A`) in the top bar across parameters and custom movement curves.
- Shared studio `hba::dsp` curve and lane-math engine primitives preserving sample-exact audio regression fixtures and zero audio-thread allocations.
