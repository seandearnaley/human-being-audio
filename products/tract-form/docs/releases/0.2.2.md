## Tract Form 0.2.2

- Try the new Buchla and Clip wavefolder shapes with the West Coast Reese and Iron Clip presets.
- The wavefolder uses second-order antiderivative antialiasing and aligns the dry and folded signals when you blend them with Warp.
- This is the first immutable Tract Form release. Its macOS, Windows and Ubuntu packages can be verified against the published release.
