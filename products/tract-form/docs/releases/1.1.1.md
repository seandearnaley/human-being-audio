# Tract Form 1.1.1

Closing the editor on Windows now releases the renderer before its native parent window.

- **4× Anti-Aliased Growl Clipper & Clean Analog Carrier Core:** Upgraded the growl ceiling clipper from 2× memoryless soft-clipping to a 4× HIIR (`192 kHz`, 144 dB stopband) ADAA1 clipper (`hba::dsp::BaseRate4x` + `hba::dsp::SoftClipAdaa1`) paired with a Nyquist-guarded, full double-precision Bessel-windowed analog sawtooth carrier (`Osc Shape`) that eliminates upper-mid fuzz and locks every harmonic cleanly under `Beat Lock`.
- **Free Hz LFOs with Exact Linked Follow & Ratio:** Added `Free` (monotonic Hz, BPM-independent) and `Free R` (note-retriggered Hz) modes (`0.05–20.0 Hz`) to all four movement lanes (`hba::dsp::lanePhaseHz`). Followers automatically lock to a Free leader's Hz clock at exact `÷4 .. ×4` ratios without cumulative drift, with live 4-second time-window visualization in `StudioMovementPanel`.
- **Speaker-Ready Rolling Reese Repertoire (+8 Presets, 50 Total):** Expanded `Sub Warmth` bloom and added 8 new Rolling Reese & Free-Linked-LFO presets (`Liquid Velvet Roller`, `Bristol Chest Reese`, `Speaker Ready Reese`, `Midnight Drift Roller`, `Free Choir Reese`, `Liquid Phaser Roller`, `Dark Techstep Roller`, `Analog Glide Roller`) voiced for warm, alias-free translation on both club subs and small speakers without harsh OTT pumping.
