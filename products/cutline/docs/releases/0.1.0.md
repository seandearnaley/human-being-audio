## Cutline 0.1.0

- First release of Cutline: an expressive phrase-grammar, gravitational microtiming, and MPE/CC MIDI articulator inspired by Nick Collins' BBCut.
- Five deterministic `(seed, phrase)` grammars (`Neuro`, `BBCut11`, `WarpCut`, `Halftime`, `Liquid`), strictly monotonic continuous gravitational microtiming across four metric pockets, four tempo-locked Bézier expression lanes (`Pitch Bend`, `CC74 Slide`, `CC1 Mod`, `Pressure`, `CC11`, `CC2`, `CC71`, `Velocity`), and three output modes (`Mono Ch1`, `MPE Lower Zone`, `Drum Kit`).
- Includes Live MIDI re-phrasing, one-drag 960-PPQ `.mid` phrase capture into any DAW, optional built-in `hba::dsp` audition voice, 24 factory presets, and VST3, macOS Audio Unit (`aumi` MIDI FX), and Standalone formats.
