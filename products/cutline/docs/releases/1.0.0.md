## Cutline 1.0.0

- **Tactile Phrase Stage & Chord Progression Strip:** Interactive 16-bar phrase piano roll with scale-snapped note dragging, edge duration handles, velocity/accent lane editing, clickable span locking across seed rolls, draggable fill/turnaround dividers, and a built-in Chord Progression Strip driving harmonic movement across the phrase.
- **BBCut-Inspired Phrase Grammars & Polyphonic Breaks:** Five grammars (`Neuro`, `BBCut11`, `WarpCut`, `Halftime`, `Liquid`) with `Loop` vs `Evolve` modes, six melodic contours, polyphonic chord/break voicings, 64-step `‹ Roll ›` seed history, and a strictly monotonic continuous gravitational microtiming field.
- **8-Voice PolyBLEP Internal Audition Synth & MIDI Export:** Zero-latency 8-voice stereo audition engine (`Reese`, `Sub`, `Pluck`, `Kit`) with anti-aliased PolyBLEP oscillators, one-drag 960-PPQ `.mid` export of the exact rendered phrase, and live pulsing `NOTE / PB / CC74 / CC1 / AT` output telemetry.
- **Live MIDI, MPE & 32 factory presets:** Real-time MIDI `Transpose` and `Chord Carve` with `Latch`, Ableton-safe Mono Ch 1 (with RPN `00 00` bend range), full MPE Lower Zone (Ch 2–15, ±48 st), and Drum Kit modes, four multi-hue tempo-locked Bézier expression lanes, and processor-owned Undo/Redo & A/B compare.
