## Cutline 3.0.0

- **The grid is what plays:** Every drum note comes from the steps you see. Nothing in playback is random. Steps sit in three layers (`Core`, `Groove`, `Detail`), and one `Density` knob brings the layers in from the stripped beat at 0% to everything at 100%. Fills are drawn by hand, fire every 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars, and are off until you turn them on.
- **A faster grid:** A `Core / Groove / Detail` brush, drag to paint or erase steps, `⌥`-drag to nudge a step, `⌥`-click to flam it, and a pocket setting for each lane. Marks inside each step show exactly when its hits land, including swing, ratchets and flams. `Vary` writes a new variation into the pattern, and the phrase strip shows where you are and which bars fill.
- **46 patterns and 22 presets:** Drum & bass, halftime, jungle, breaks, club and bass music patterns and 13 fills, loadable from the `Patterns` and `Fills` menus. The 13 drum presets fill all four scenes (main, variation, breakdown, peak), and there are 4 Strike Body presets and 5 MIDI Slicer presets.
- **Six PLAY programs:** `Repeat`, `Roll` (kick, snare or hat), `Density` (ride the layers live), `Rate`, `Drop` and `Fill`, all locked to the grid and engaged from the pad or from Pad MIDI keys `C3`–`F3`. The pad surface draws the notes Cutline actually sends.
- **Breaking change:** Cutline 2 presets, sessions and automation are not loaded. A Cutline 2 session opens on the default beat.
