## Cutline 0.2.0

- Inaugural public studio release of Cutline: an expressive phrase-grammar, gravitational microtiming, and MPE/CC MIDI articulator inspired by Nick Collins' BBCut.
- Five phrase grammars (`Neuro`, `BBCut11`, `WarpCut`, `Halftime`, `Liquid`) with seed-driven rhythm vocabularies, recursive splits, six melodic contours (`Auto`, `Pedal`, `Arch`, `Rise`, `Fall`, `Acid`), `Loop` vs `Evolve` motif modes, 64-step `‹ Roll ›` seed history, and a strictly monotonic continuous gravitational microtiming field.
- Direct-manipulation Phrase Stage: click spans in the Phrase Tape to Lock/Pin them across seed rolls, drag dividers to resize fill/turnaround windows, right-click spans to override roles (including `REST`), drag notes vertically snapped to scale, Alt-click to mute, inspect timing/velocity/bend in the hover micro-HUD, and monitor live MIDI output with pulsing `NOTE / PB / CC74 / CC1 / AT` indicators.
- Live MIDI `Transpose` and `Chord Carve` input modes with `Latch`, Mono Ch 1 (with RPN `00 00` pitch-bend range), MPE Lower Zone (Ch 2–15, ±48 st, scale-diatonic voicing), and Drum Kit outputs, plus four multi-hue tempo-locked Bézier expression lanes.
- 4-voice poly stereo internal audition synth (`Reese`, `Sub`, `Pluck`, `Kit`), exact current-phrase 960-PPQ `.mid` drag export, 64-step Undo/Redo (`⌘Z` / `⌘⇧Z`) and A/B Compare (`Copy A → B`), 24 factory presets, and VST3, macOS Audio Unit MIDI FX (`aumi`), and Standalone formats.
