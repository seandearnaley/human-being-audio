## Cutline 2.0.0

- **Playable Two-Surface `PLAY | GRID` Instrument:** Reimagined from the ground up as a live MIDI breakbeat, polymetric drum & pitch-transparent slicer instrument. The default **`PLAY`** landing view features a giant 60fps generative-art **2D X/Y Articulation Stage** across 8 jewel-coded worlds (`Slice`, `Roll`, `Bounce`, `Swing`, `Poly`, `Stutter`, `Morph`, `Drop` — playable via mouse, automation, or MIDI `C3–G3`), with live `Hold`, quantized switching, `XY → CC` expression output (`CC74 + CC1`, `CC74 + Vel`, `CC1 + AT`), and a clickable 8-lane live MIDI Pulse Ribbon.
- **8-Lane Polymetric Drum Matrix & 12 Breakbeat Styles (`GRID`):** 1-click toggle into 8 macro knobs (`Style`, `Gravity`, `Ghosts`, `Chop`, `Ratchet`, `Complex`, `Fills`, `Dynamics`), per-lane polymetric loop lengths (`1–32` steps), step painting, velocity sculpting, ratchets (`×2/×3/×4`), grace flams, Euclidean overrides, Mute/Solo/Lock, 4 switchable Scenes (`A–D`), `Mutate` / `Polymeter` / `Clone →`, and 2 optional CC/Velocity expression curve lanes (zero default Pitch Bend).
- **10 External Drum VST Kit Maps + Global `0.5× / 1× / 2×` Rate:** Out-of-the-box MIDI mappings for `Live Rack / GM`, `Strike Body`, `Chromatic`, `Addictive Drums 2`, `Toontrack (SD3/EZD)`, `BFD3`, `GetGood Drums`, `Slate SSD5`, `NI Studio / Abbey`, and `XLN XO` with dynamic kit-aware lane labels and per-lane custom note overrides, plus instant automatable Half-Time (`0.5×`), Normal (`1×`), and Double-Time (`2×`) scaling synced across playback, playhead, and `.mid` export.
- **Pitch-Transparent MIDI Slicer & 32 Curated Presets:** `Stab Chop`, `Strum / Flam`, `Break Arp`, `Bass / Stab`, and `Clip Slice` (live 1-bar incoming MIDI ring-buffer re-slicer) slice your played chords and basslines without inventing random notes. Includes an upgraded 8-voice electronic drum & clean stab audition engine, 960-PPQ drag-and-drop `.mid` capture, 32 curated presets across 6 categories, and full `[[clang::nonblocking]]` + RealtimeSanitizer safety.
