# Lineage 1.0.0

Initial release of Lineage — Human Being Audio's non-destructive resampling Reese and bass sculpting synthesizer.

## Highlights

- **6 Zero-Aliasing Bass Cores** (`Analog Reese`, `Soft Sync`, `Hard Sync`, `TZFM`, `Pulse Reese`, `Sub Fold`) with 7-voice unison and clean phase-locked Sub Lock + small-speaker harmonic translation.
- **Serial Nonlinear Sculpting Bench**: Filter 1 (`LP24`, `BP12`, `Notch`, `Comb`, `Formant`) → 5-model ADAA2 Shaper → Filter 2 (`Notch`, `Dual Notch`, `LP12`, `BP12`, `Phaser`) → Elastic Dispersion → 3-Band Controlled Density inside 2×/4×/8× HIIR oversampling.
- **Non-Destructive Multi-Generation Resampling (Strata)**: Internal lock-free triple-buffered resampling (`PRINT GEN` & `STACK +1`) with key-tracked pitch playback, loop scrubbing, Ping-Pong/Reverse/Scrub directions, external WAV drop/load, and 16× offline WAV export.
- **Sculptural Organic PLAY & STUDIO Surfaces**: 5 interactive 60 fps Visage performance worlds (`Sculpt`, `Reese`, `Strata`, `Disperse`, `Abyss`) + deep STUDIO deck with unified 4-lane Sync/Retrig/1-Shot/Free-Hz movement curves and 64-step Undo/Redo + A/B compare.
