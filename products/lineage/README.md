# Lineage

Non-destructive resampling Reese & bass sculpting synthesizer, by Human Being Audio.

Lineage pairs a zero-aliasing 7-voice analog Reese, soft/hard sync, and through-zero FM oscillator core with a serial nonlinear bass-sculpting bench (Filter 1 → ADAA2 Shaper → Dual-Notch/Phaser → Elastic Dispersion → 3-Band Controlled Density) and a live, non-destructive multi-generation resampling engine (**Strata**). Print a 1–4 bar bass pass internally in milliseconds, play it chromatically so beating and filter sweeps pitch-scale organically, scrub or stack generations (`Gen 1 → Gen 2 → Gen 3`), and keep a clean phase-locked mono sub anchored to the live played note while every upstream knob stays editable.

## Highlights

- **Gen 0 · Zero-Aliasing Reese & Sync Core**: 6 oscillator engines (`Analog Reese`, `Soft Sync`, `Hard Sync`, `TZFM`, `Pulse Reese`, `Sub Fold`), up to 7-voice stereo unison, and a dedicated phase-locked mono sub with small-speaker 2nd/3rd harmonic warmth.
- **Gen 1 · Serial Sculpting Bench**: Cytomic TPT SVF + Comb/Formant Filter 1, 5-model ADAA2 waveshaper (`Tube`, `Fold`, `Buchla`, `Sine`, `Clip`) with gain compensation, post-drive Dual-Notch/Phaser Filter 2, 6-stage allpass Dispersion, and phase-coherent 3-band Density.
- **Gen 2 · Live Non-Destructive Resampling (Strata)**: 1-click internal resampling (`PRINT GEN`) & multi-generation stacking (`STACK +1`) with Hermite key-tracked playback, loop-window scrubbing, Ping-Pong/Reverse/Scrub modes, external WAV loading, and instant 16× drag-to-DAW WAV export.
- **Sculptural PLAY & STUDIO UI**: 5 living 60 fps organic X/Y performance worlds (`Sculpt`, `Reese`, `Strata`, `Disperse`, `Abyss`) with touch-to-play and `Space` Drone, plus a 1-click STUDIO deck with interactive filter/strata canvases and the unified 4-lane Free-Hz movement engine.
