## Long (about 200 words)

Tract Form is a monophonic bass synthesizer for neurofunk and drum & bass. It
starts from a simple idea: take a clean sub and grow the rest of the sound out
of it, without ever letting the rest disturb it.

One master sine sets the pitch. It phase-modulates two reese voices, and it
also becomes a mono sub that bypasses every stage of processing. The growl has
its own ceiling under the sub, so however hard you drive it, the low end holds
its level.

Because the growl is built on harmonics of the note, its controls are too.
Reach sets the highest harmonic, so brightness follows the pitch. Weight leans
the spectrum towards the bottom. Sculpt places up to six notches or peaks on
chosen harmonics, and stays in tune across the keyboard. Beat lock makes the two
voices beat once per note division, for a reese that rolls in time. A
five-formant vowel filter and an anti-aliased wave folder give it a voice.

Four tempo-locked movement lanes each play a curve you draw, aimed at any of
ten parameters. A lane can follow another at a ratio, so one gesture can drive
several parts of the sound at once.

Tract Form is open source and runs as VST3, Audio Unit and Standalone on macOS.
